In Warhammer 40,000 (10th Edition Core Rules), using a Stratagem requires spending Command points   
(CP) and following the specific instructions and restrictions printed on the Stratagem's card.      

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Acquiring and Paying Command Points (CP)                                                         

 • Gaining CP: At the start of your Command phase, before doing anything else, both players gain 1CP
   [Core Rules p. 11]. Outside of the CP gained at the start of the Command phase, each player can  
   only gain a maximum of 1CP per battle round, regardless of the source [Core Rules p. 11].        
 • Paying CP Cost: Each Stratagem has a listed CP cost (e.g., 1CP) [Core Rules p. 41–42]. When you  
   use a Stratagem, you must reduce your CP total by the amount listed on that Stratagem [Core Rules
   p. 41].                                                                                          
 • CP Sufficiency: If you do not have enough CP to pay the required cost for a specific Stratagem,  
   you cannot use it [Core Rules p. 41].                                                            

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2. General Usage Rules & Limits                                                                     

 • Availability: All players can use the Core Stratagems found in the rulebook, and additional      
   Stratagems can be found in Codexes and other publications [Core Rules p. 41].                    
 • Phase Limit: You can use the same Stratagem multiple times over the course of a battle, but you  
   cannot use the same Stratagem more than once in the same phase [Core Rules p. 41].               
 • Battle-shock Restriction: While a unit is Battle-shocked, its controlling player cannot use      
   Stratagems to affect that unit [Core Rules p. 11] (unless an explicit exception applies on the   
   Stratagem itself, such as Insane Bravery targeting the unit that just tested [Core Rules p. 42]).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3. How to Read and Execute Stratagem Cards                                                          

Stratagem cards define their execution through labeled sections [Core Rules p. 41–42]:              

 • WHEN (Timing): Specifies the exact phase, player turn, or trigger during which the Stratagem can 
   be used.                                                                                         
    • Example (Core Rules p. 42, Fire Overwatch): "Your opponent's Movement or Charge phase, just   
      after an enemy unit is set up or when an enemy unit starts or ends a Normal, Advance, Fall    
      Back or Charge move."                                                                         
    • Example (Core Rules p. 41, Command Re-roll): "In any phase, just after you have made a Hit    
      roll, a Wound roll, a Damage roll, a saving throw, an Advance roll, a Charge roll, a Desperate
      Escape test, a Hazardous test, or just after you have rolled the dice to determine the number 
      of attacks made with a weapon..."                                                             
 • TARGET (Eligibility): Identifies the specific model or unit from your army (or in Reserves)      
   eligible to receive the effect, including any positional requirements or exclusions.             
    • Example (Core Rules p. 42, Grenade): "One Grenades unit from your army that is not within     
      Engagement Range of any enemy units and has not been selected to shoot this phase."           
 • EFFECT (Result): Describes the rule, action, or benefit applied once the CP is paid and targets  
   are chosen.                                                                                      
    • Example (Core Rules p. 41, Command Re-roll): "You re-roll that roll, test or saving throw."   
    • Example (Core Rules p. 42, Grenade): You select an eligible visible enemy unit within 8" not  
      in Engagement Range, roll six D6, and each 4+ inflicts 1 mortal wound.                        
 • RESTRICTIONS (Limitations): Details any special prohibitions, usage caps, or rule modifications  
   that constrain the Stratagem.                                                                    
    • Example (Core Rules p. 42, Fire Overwatch): Until the end of the phase, ranged attacks require
      an unmodified Hit roll of 6 to hit regardless of Ballistic Skill or modifiers, and the player 
      can only use that Stratagem once per turn.                                                    
    • Example [DB: stratagems, publication/edition not recorded]: Certain versions of Insane Bravery
      specify that the Stratagem cannot be used more than once per battle.
